# YouTube Artifact Storage Policy

For every video, preserve:
1. Story/script
2. Quran/Hadith/reference verification record
3. Urdu voice/audio
4. Nature background/source record
5. Subtitle/on-screen text data
6. Thumbnail
7. Final rendered video
8. Metadata/manifest
9. Human approval status

Folder pattern:
youtube/stories/<category>/
youtube/audio/<category>/
youtube/thumbnails/<category>/
youtube/videos/<category>/
youtube/backgrounds/nature/
youtube/manifests/

Categories: neki, namaz, quran, hadith.

Every generated video uses Hidayat Tube Official branding and Like/Subscribe CTA.

YouTube publishing remains OFF until explicit human approval.
