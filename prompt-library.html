/adcinematic
v3
Production fields
purpose

advertisement-style polished cinematic product shot بنانا.

prompt

Create an 8-second cinematic advertisement shot of [SUBJECT] in [SCENE], using clear product hierarchy, polished composition, and concise visual storytelling suitable for commercial presentation.

camera

Stabilized commercial camera with deliberate framing.

motion

Controlled product or camera movement.

lighting

Clean commercial lighting with strong subject separation.

avoid

No invented claims, fake text, logos, or product deformation.

format

8 seconds, 16:9 or 9:16 as requested.

/beach
v3
Production fields
purpose

cinematic beach environment اور shoreline motion دکھانا.

prompt

Create an 8-second cinematic beach shot of [SCENE], featuring realistic shoreline, sand texture, atmospheric depth, and gentle water movement.

camera

Stabilized wide camera near shoreline.

motion

Slow lateral track or gentle push.

lighting

Natural daylight with soft reflected fill.

avoid

No repeated wave patterns, floating sand, or warped horizon.

format

8 seconds, 16:9 by default; 9:16 when requested.
