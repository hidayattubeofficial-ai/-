# Hidayat Video Command Library v2 — Batch 08: Product & Commercial

## Status
LOCKED v2 category — 6 commands

## Canonical schema
Command → Purpose → Prompt → Camera → Motion → Lighting → Avoid → Format

## 1. /producthero
**Purpose:** product کو premium hero composition میں مرکزی فوکس کے ساتھ نمایاں کرنا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second premium hero shot of [SUBJECT] in [SCENE], presenting the product as the clear visual focus with precise composition, realistic materials, controlled reflections, and polished commercial detail.
**Camera:** Slow stabilized push-in or three-quarter product angle.
**Motion:** Minimal product movement with controlled camera motion.
**Lighting:** Clean studio-style key, soft fill, and subtle rim separation.
**Avoid:** No invented logos, no fake text, no altered product shape, no floating product, no material deformation.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 9:16 by default; 16:9 when requested.

## 2. /product360
**Purpose:** product کا controlled 360-degree showcase بنانا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second controlled 360-degree product showcase of [SUBJECT] in [SCENE], rotating the product or camera smoothly to reveal its complete form while preserving exact proportions, materials, and visible details.
**Camera:** Stable product turntable or synchronized orbit camera.
**Motion:** Continuous smooth rotation at constant speed.
**Lighting:** Consistent studio lighting with realistic reflection movement.
**Avoid:** No geometry changes, no duplicated product parts, no invented logos or text, no flicker.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 16:9 or 9:16 as requested.

## 3. /productmacro
**Purpose:** product کی fine details اور material quality کو macro cinematic shot میں دکھانا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second macro commercial shot of [SUBJECT] in [SCENE], focusing on fine material texture, craftsmanship, edges, or functional details with realistic depth and precise surface rendering.
**Camera:** Macro lens with controlled slider movement.
**Motion:** Slow lateral slide or micro push-in.
**Lighting:** Soft directional highlights designed to reveal texture without glare.
**Avoid:** No fake micro-text, no melted edges, no exaggerated texture, no invented branding.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 9:16 by default; 16:9 when requested.

## 4. /productfloating
**Purpose:** product کو clean premium floating composition میں visually isolate کرنا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second premium commercial shot of [SUBJECT] appearing visually suspended against [SCENE], using a clean controlled composition while preserving realistic product geometry, materials, and lighting.
**Camera:** Stable centered framing with a subtle orbit or push.
**Motion:** Slow controlled rotation or camera movement; product remains visually stable.
**Lighting:** Soft studio key with controlled rim and floor/background separation.
**Avoid:** No visible support unless requested, no warped product, no invented logos, no unrealistic reflections.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 9:16 by default; 16:9 when requested.

## 5. /productlifestyle
**Purpose:** product کو natural lifestyle environment میں aspirational مگر realistic انداز میں پیش کرنا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic lifestyle commercial featuring [SUBJECT] naturally integrated into [SCENE], with believable human-scale interaction, realistic materials, and an uncluttered premium composition.
**Camera:** Stabilized handheld or slow dolly at natural eye level.
**Motion:** Subtle environmental and product interaction movement.
**Lighting:** Motivated natural or soft commercial lighting.
**Avoid:** No invented branding, no fake text, no unnatural product placement, no distorted hands or objects.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 9:16 by default; 16:9 when requested.

## 6. /productreveal
**Purpose:** product کو controlled reveal movement کے ذریعے cinematic طور پر سامنے لانا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic product reveal of [SUBJECT] in [SCENE], beginning with a partial obstruction or detail and smoothly revealing the complete product with precise framing and realistic material response.
**Camera:** Slow dolly, slider, or controlled arc reveal.
**Motion:** Continuous reveal with smooth acceleration and deceleration.
**Lighting:** Directional key light that gradually reveals product contours.
**Avoid:** No visible cut, no product morphing, no invented logos or text, no exposure jump.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 9:16 by default; 16:9 when requested.
