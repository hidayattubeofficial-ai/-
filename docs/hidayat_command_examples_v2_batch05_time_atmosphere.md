# Hidayat Video Command Library v2 — Batch 05: Time & Atmosphere

## Status
LOCKED v2 category — 7 commands

## Canonical schema
Command → Purpose → Prompt → Camera → Motion → Lighting → Avoid → Format

## 1. /goldenhour
**Purpose:** نرم warm sunlight اور long shadows کے ساتھ golden-hour cinematic mood بنانا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic shot of [SUBJECT] in [SCENE] during golden hour, with warm low-angle sunlight, long natural shadows, soft atmospheric depth, and realistic surface response.
**Camera:** Slow stabilized dolly or gentle three-quarter orbit.
**Motion:** Smooth restrained camera movement with natural subject motion.
**Lighting:** Warm low-angle key light with subtle rim illumination.
**Avoid:** No excessive orange tint, no clipped highlights, no artificial glow, no inconsistent shadow direction.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 9:16 by default; 16:9 when requested.

## 2. /bluehour
**Purpose:** sunset کے فوراً بعد blue-hour کی cool cinematic atmosphere پیدا کرنا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic blue-hour shot of [SUBJECT] in [SCENE], balancing cool ambient sky light with subtle practical illumination and realistic dusk exposure.
**Camera:** Stabilized medium-wide framing with a slow push-in.
**Motion:** Gentle environmental movement and restrained camera drift.
**Lighting:** Cool blue ambient light with warm practical accents where motivated.
**Avoid:** No artificial neon cast unless requested, no crushed shadows, no exposure pumping.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 16:9 by default; 9:16 when requested.

## 3. /midnight
**Purpose:** deep-night ماحول میں controlled low-key cinematic mood بنانا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic midnight shot of [SUBJECT] in [SCENE], using deep low-key contrast, sparse practical lights, realistic night visibility, and atmospheric depth.
**Camera:** Slow stabilized push-in or lateral track.
**Motion:** Subtle natural movement with deliberate cinematic pacing.
**Lighting:** Motivated practicals and soft cool ambient fill.
**Avoid:** No daylight-level illumination, no crushed subject detail, no random colored lights, no artificial fog unless requested.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 9:16 by default; 16:9 when requested.

## 4. /dawn
**Purpose:** صبح کے آغاز کی soft light اور gradually brightening atmosphere کو cinematic بنانا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic dawn shot of [SUBJECT] in [SCENE], with soft early-morning light, gentle atmospheric haze, and a gradual transition from cool shadow into warmer daylight.
**Camera:** Slow crane, dolly, or stabilized reveal.
**Motion:** Calm natural environmental movement.
**Lighting:** Soft low-angle dawn light with cool residual shadows.
**Avoid:** No instant sunrise, no unrealistic color shift, no exposure jump, no inconsistent shadow direction.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 16:9 by default; 9:16 when requested.

## 5. /overcast
**Purpose:** cloud-covered soft light میں even exposure اور atmospheric cinematic realism پیدا کرنا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic overcast shot of [SUBJECT] in [SCENE], with broad diffused sky illumination, soft shadows, muted highlights, and realistic atmospheric depth.
**Camera:** Stabilized medium or wide composition with subtle movement.
**Motion:** Gentle tracking or slow push.
**Lighting:** Large diffused overhead source resembling natural overcast daylight.
**Avoid:** No hard sunlight, no artificial HDR glow, no harsh shadow edges, no exposure pumping.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 16:9 or 9:16 as requested.

## 6. /mistymorning
**Purpose:** صبح کی ہلکی دھند اور soft atmospheric layers کے ساتھ dreamy مگر realistic منظر بنانا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic misty-morning shot of [SUBJECT] in [SCENE], with layered low-density mist, soft morning illumination, realistic depth falloff, and clear subject separation.
**Camera:** Slow stabilized push-in or lateral reveal.
**Motion:** Subtle drifting mist and restrained environmental motion.
**Lighting:** Soft diffused morning light with gentle backlight through the mist.
**Avoid:** No opaque fog wall, no subject disappearance, no artificial glow, no random mist direction changes.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 16:9 by default; 9:16 when requested.

## 7. /moonlight
**Purpose:** چاندنی رات کی cool directional light اور cinematic night atmosphere بنانا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic moonlit shot of [SUBJECT] in [SCENE], using cool directional moonlight, subtle ambient fill, realistic night exposure, and restrained atmospheric depth.
**Camera:** Slow stabilized dolly or three-quarter orbit.
**Motion:** Gentle subject/environment movement with smooth camera motion.
**Lighting:** Cool moonlight key with soft shadow detail and minimal practical accents.
**Avoid:** No daylight-level brightness, no oversized visible moon unless requested, no random colored lighting, no crushed subject detail.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 16:9 or 9:16 as requested.
