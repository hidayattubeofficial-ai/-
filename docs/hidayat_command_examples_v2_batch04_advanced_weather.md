# Hidayat Video Command Library v2 — Batch 04: Advanced Weather

## Status
LOCKED v2 category — 6 commands

## Canonical schema
Command → Purpose → Prompt → Camera → Motion → Lighting → Avoid → Format

## 1. /stormfront
**Purpose:** طوفانی بادلوں اور dramatic weather front کے ساتھ cinematic ماحول بنانا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic shot of [SUBJECT] in [SCENE] as a realistic storm front approaches, with layered dark clouds, controlled wind, distant rain, and atmospheric depth while preserving subject continuity.
**Camera:** Wide stabilized cinematic framing with a slow push-in.
**Motion:** Natural wind-driven environmental motion and subtle camera movement.
**Lighting:** Dramatic diffused daylight with intermittent distant highlights.
**Avoid:** No supernatural weather, no random lightning strikes, no exposure flicker, no subject deformation.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 16:9 by default; 9:16 when requested.

## 2. /heavyrain
**Purpose:** شدید بارش، water streaks اور wet reflections کے ساتھ realistic cinematic scene بنانا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic shot of [SUBJECT] in [SCENE] during heavy realistic rainfall, with visible rain depth, wet surfaces, natural runoff, and coherent atmospheric perspective.
**Camera:** Stabilized medium or wide cinematic camera.
**Motion:** Gentle tracking or slow push while rain falls consistently through the frame.
**Lighting:** Soft overcast or motivated night lighting reflected naturally on wet surfaces.
**Avoid:** No uniform rain lines, no floating water, no mirror-like surfaces without cause, no exposure pumping.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 16:9 or 9:16 as requested.

## 3. /blizzard
**Purpose:** برفانی طوفان میں visibility، snow accumulation اور atmospheric motion کو cinematic بنانا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic blizzard scene featuring [SUBJECT] in [SCENE], with dense layered snowfall, wind-driven snow, realistic accumulation, and reduced visibility while keeping the main subject readable.
**Camera:** Stabilized medium-wide camera with restrained movement.
**Motion:** Strong directional snow and subtle environmental movement.
**Lighting:** Cold diffused daylight with soft contrast.
**Avoid:** No snow clipping through solid objects, no duplicated flakes forming patterns, no subject distortion, no impossible visibility.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 16:9 by default; 9:16 when requested.

## 4. /sandstorm
**Purpose:** ریت کے طوفان میں atmospheric haze، wind اور reduced visibility کا realistic اثر پیدا کرنا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic sandstorm shot of [SUBJECT] in [SCENE], with layered airborne dust, strong directional wind, realistic visibility falloff, and grounded environmental interaction.
**Camera:** Stabilized low-to-medium wide camera.
**Motion:** Controlled camera drift with wind-driven dust crossing the frame.
**Lighting:** Warm diffused sunlight filtered through airborne dust.
**Avoid:** No solid-looking dust walls, no subject duplication, no impossible wind direction, no exposure flicker.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 16:9 by default; 9:16 when requested.

## 5. /hailstorm
**Purpose:** ژالہ باری کو realistic impact، surface interaction اور cinematic weather detail کے ساتھ دکھانا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic hailstorm shot of [SUBJECT] in [SCENE], showing varied hailstones falling with realistic speed, bounce, and interaction with visible surfaces while maintaining safe visual clarity.
**Camera:** Stabilized medium-wide camera with optional slow push.
**Motion:** Natural hail trajectories and believable surface impacts.
**Lighting:** Overcast storm lighting with soft directional highlights.
**Avoid:** No oversized hailstones, no repeated identical trajectories, no floating impacts, no subject damage unless explicitly requested.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 16:9 or 9:16 as requested.

## 6. /rainbowafterstorm
**Purpose:** طوفان کے بعد clear atmosphere، wet surfaces اور natural rainbow کے ذریعے hopeful cinematic منظر بنانا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic post-storm shot of [SUBJECT] in [SCENE], with clearing clouds, wet reflective surfaces, fresh atmospheric depth, and a subtle physically motivated rainbow appearing through the remaining moisture.
**Camera:** Slow stabilized reveal or gentle crane movement.
**Motion:** Light residual water movement and gradual cloud clearing.
**Lighting:** Warm sunlight breaking through cooler residual clouds.
**Avoid:** No artificial rainbow saturation, no impossible rainbow position, no sudden weather jump, no exposure flash.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 16:9 by default; 9:16 when requested.
