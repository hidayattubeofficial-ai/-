# Hidayat Video Command Library v2 — Batch 06: Nature & Environment

## Status
LOCKED v2 category — 10 commands

## Canonical schema
Command → Purpose → Prompt → Camera → Motion → Lighting → Avoid → Format

## 1. /forestcinematic
**Purpose:** جنگل کے layered depth اور natural atmosphere کے ساتھ cinematic منظر بنانا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic shot of [SUBJECT] moving through a dense natural forest in [SCENE], with layered foliage, realistic depth, subtle environmental movement, and grounded spatial continuity.
**Camera:** Stabilized slow tracking camera at human height.
**Motion:** Gentle forward movement with natural leaves and branches responding to air movement.
**Lighting:** Soft filtered daylight through the canopy.
**Avoid:** No repeating foliage patterns, no floating vegetation, no artificial fog unless requested, no subject deformation.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 16:9 by default; 9:16 when requested.

## 2. /waterfall
**Purpose:** آبشار کی flowing water dynamics اور surrounding landscape کو cinematic انداز میں دکھانا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic waterfall shot in [SCENE], featuring [SUBJECT] near a naturally flowing waterfall with realistic water volume, mist, rocks, and environmental scale.
**Camera:** Slow stabilized lateral move or gentle push-in.
**Motion:** Continuous water flow with natural splash and mist movement.
**Lighting:** Soft daylight with controlled highlights on wet surfaces.
**Avoid:** No frozen water, no repeating splash patterns, no plastic-looking rocks, no impossible water direction.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 16:9 by default; 9:16 when requested.

## 10. /naturetimelapse
**Purpose:** قدرتی ماحول کی slow temporal change کو compact cinematic sequence میں دکھانا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic nature time-lapse of [SCENE] featuring [SUBJECT], showing a coherent accelerated progression of clouds, changing light, vegetation movement, or other natural environmental changes while preserving spatial continuity.
**Camera:** Locked or extremely stable tripod composition.
**Motion:** Accelerated environmental change with consistent camera framing.
**Lighting:** Gradual physically plausible change in natural illumination.
**Avoid:** No teleporting clouds, no discontinuous shadows, no duplicated objects, no sudden scene redesign.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 16:9 by default; 9:16 when requested.
