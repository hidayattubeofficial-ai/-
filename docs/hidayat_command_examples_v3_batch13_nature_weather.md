## 1. /ocean
**Purpose:** وسیع cinematic ocean environment دکھانا.
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic ocean environment shot of [SCENE], featuring [SUBJECT] with realistic water scale, horizon depth, and natural surface motion.
**Camera:** Stabilized wide camera with gentle lateral movement.
**Motion:** Coherent waves and environmental motion.
**Lighting:** Natural daylight or motivated atmospheric light.
**Avoid:** No tiled waves, impossible horizon, or duplicated objects.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 16:9 by default; 9:16 when requested.
