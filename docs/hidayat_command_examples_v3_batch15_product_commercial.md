## Schema
Command → Purpose → Prompt → Camera → Motion → Lighting → Avoid → Format

## 1. /productrotate
**Purpose:** product کی controlled rotational showcase بنانا.
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second controlled product rotation of [SUBJECT] in [SCENE], rotating the product smoothly to reveal its form while preserving exact geometry, materials, and visible details.
**Camera:** Stable turntable or synchronized orbit camera.
**Motion:** Constant smooth product rotation.
**Lighting:** Consistent studio or motivated commercial lighting.
**Avoid:** No geometry changes, duplicated parts, invented logos, or fake text.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 16:9 or 9:16 as requested.
