# Hidayat Video Command Library v2 — Batch 09: Social Media

## Status
LOCKED v2 category — 7 commands

## Canonical schema
Command → Purpose → Prompt → Camera → Motion → Lighting → Avoid → Format

## 1. /shorts
**Purpose:** YouTube Shorts کے لیے فوری، vertical اور high-retention cinematic shot بنانا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second vertical short-form video of [SUBJECT] in [SCENE], with an immediate visual hook, clear subject focus, strong composition, and fast but coherent cinematic pacing designed for short-form viewing.
**Camera:** Dynamic stabilized vertical framing with a strong opening composition.
**Motion:** Immediate subject or camera movement that establishes the visual hook early.
**Lighting:** Clean high-contrast lighting with clear subject separation.
**Avoid:** No slow empty opening, no cluttered composition, no unreadable subject, no fake text or invented branding.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 9:16 vertical, short-form optimized.

## 2. /reels
**Purpose:** Instagram Reels کے لیے polished vertical cinematic content تیار کرنا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second polished vertical reel featuring [SUBJECT] in [SCENE], using an instantly readable composition, smooth cinematic motion, and visually engaging pacing suitable for mobile viewing.
**Camera:** Stabilized vertical medium or close framing.
**Motion:** Smooth subject action with controlled camera movement.
**Lighting:** Clean lifestyle or commercial lighting with natural contrast.
**Avoid:** No excessive empty space, no distracting background elements, no identity or product distortion, no invented text.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 9:16 vertical.

## 3. /tiktok
**Purpose:** TikTok کے لیے fast, engaging اور visually immediate vertical shot بنانا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second TikTok-style vertical video of [SUBJECT] in [SCENE], opening with an immediate visual action or transformation while maintaining realistic motion, clear framing, and strong mobile-first composition.
**Camera:** Energetic stabilized vertical camera with a close or medium starting frame.
**Motion:** Fast readable movement with a clean visual payoff.
**Lighting:** Bright, clear, high-separation lighting appropriate for mobile viewing.
**Avoid:** No confusing opening, no chaotic camera shake, no anatomy distortion, no fake logos or text.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 9:16 vertical.

## 4. /loop
**Purpose:** ایسا seamless short video بنانا جو شروع اور اختتام پر قدرتی طور پر repeat ہو سکے۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second seamless looping video of [SUBJECT] in [SCENE], designing the beginning and ending frames so the motion connects naturally with no visible jump when repeated.
**Camera:** Locked or smoothly stabilized composition that supports loop continuity.
**Motion:** Continuous cyclical movement with matching start and end states.
**Lighting:** Stable lighting throughout the entire loop.
**Avoid:** No visible reset, no jump cut, no exposure change, no subject morphing, no discontinuity.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 9:16 by default; 1:1 or 16:9 when requested.

## 5. /satisfying
**Purpose:** visually satisfying، precise اور smooth action کو cinematic انداز میں نمایاں کرنا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second visually satisfying cinematic shot of [SUBJECT] performing a precise, clean action in [SCENE], emphasizing smooth repetition, symmetry, texture, timing, and a clear visual payoff.
**Camera:** Stable close-up or controlled macro-style framing.
**Motion:** Precise rhythmic movement with clean timing.
**Lighting:** Soft directional light emphasizing texture, edges, and surface detail.
**Avoid:** No messy motion, no irregular unexplained deformation, no distracting background, no artificial texture artifacts.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 9:16 by default; 16:9 or 1:1 when requested.

## 6. /trendstyle
**Purpose:** موجودہ short-form visual style کے مطابق adaptable مگر canonical cinematic composition بنانا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second contemporary short-form video of [SUBJECT] in [SCENE], using a current social-first visual rhythm, bold readable composition, concise camera movement, and polished cinematic presentation without adding unrequested branded elements.
**Camera:** Modern stabilized vertical framing with deliberate close-to-medium composition.
**Motion:** Concise energetic movement synchronized to the visual beat.
**Lighting:** Clean contemporary lighting with strong subject separation.
**Avoid:** No copied creator identity, no invented logos, no fake captions, no excessive effects, no random trend-specific elements unless requested.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 9:16 vertical by default.

## 7. /thumbnailshot
**Purpose:** video thumbnail یا cover کے لیے فوراً سمجھ آنے والا strong hero frame بنانا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic thumbnail-focused shot of [SUBJECT] in [SCENE], building toward a single exceptionally clear hero composition with strong subject separation, readable silhouette, and visually striking framing suitable for a thumbnail extraction.
**Camera:** Stable hero framing with deliberate close or medium composition.
**Motion:** Minimal controlled movement that resolves into a strong still-like hero frame.
**Lighting:** High-separation key and rim lighting with controlled highlights.
**Avoid:** No clutter behind the subject, no face or product distortion, no fake text, no invented logos, no unreadable composition.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 16:9 by default for thumbnail extraction; 9:16 when requested.
