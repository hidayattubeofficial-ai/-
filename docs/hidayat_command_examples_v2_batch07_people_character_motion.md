# Hidayat Video Command Library v2 — Batch 07: People & Character Motion

## Status
LOCKED v2 category — 9 commands

## Canonical schema
Command → Purpose → Prompt → Camera → Motion → Lighting → Avoid → Format

## 1. /walkcycle
**Purpose:** کردار کی قدرتی walking motion کو smooth اور consistent cinematic انداز میں دکھانا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic shot of [SUBJECT] walking naturally through [SCENE], with a consistent gait, believable foot contact, coordinated arm movement, and stable body proportions.
**Camera:** Stabilized medium tracking camera at natural eye level.
**Motion:** Smooth forward walking with realistic stride and weight transfer.
**Lighting:** Motivated scene lighting with consistent highlights and shadows.
**Avoid:** No sliding feet, no duplicated limbs, no anatomy distortion, no identity morphing.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 9:16 by default; 16:9 when requested.

## 2. /runningaction
**Purpose:** کردار کی dynamic running movement کو realistic cinematic energy کے ساتھ دکھانا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic action shot of [SUBJECT] running through [SCENE], with believable stride mechanics, natural arm swing, foot contact, and controlled forward momentum.
**Camera:** Low stabilized tracking camera.
**Motion:** Dynamic but physically coherent running motion.
**Lighting:** Directional cinematic light with stable exposure.
**Avoid:** No floating feet, no extra limbs, no impossible acceleration, no identity morphing.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 9:16 by default; 16:9 when requested.

## 3. /turnandreveal
**Purpose:** کردار کے turn کرنے کے ساتھ controlled cinematic reveal پیدا کرنا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic shot of [SUBJECT] in [SCENE], beginning from a partial profile and smoothly turning to reveal the face or primary subject orientation while preserving identity, clothing, and body proportions.
**Camera:** Medium stabilized three-quarter framing.
**Motion:** Smooth subject turn with subtle camera adjustment.
**Lighting:** Soft directional key light with consistent facial exposure.
**Avoid:** No face morphing, no clothing changes, no anatomy distortion, no abrupt pose jump.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 9:16 by default; 16:9 when requested.

**Motion:** Subtle wind-driven hair and fabric movement.
**Lighting:** Soft directional portrait lighting.
**Avoid:** No extreme hair deformation, no clothing redesign, no identity change, no anatomy distortion.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 9:16 by default; 16:9 when requested.

## 6. /crowdwalk
**Purpose:** crowd کے درمیان individual character اور group movement کو coherent بنانا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic crowd scene with [SUBJECT] moving naturally through [SCENE], maintaining clear subject identity while surrounding people move independently with varied realistic walking behavior.
**Camera:** Stabilized tracking camera following the primary subject.
**Motion:** Primary subject maintains a clear trajectory while crowd motion remains varied and spatially coherent.
**Lighting:** Consistent environmental lighting across foreground and background people.
**Avoid:** No cloned crowd patterns, no duplicated faces, no merged bodies, no identity morphing.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 16:9 by default; 9:16 when requested.

## 7. /sitandstand
**Purpose:** بیٹھنے اور کھڑے ہونے کی complete body mechanics کو realistic cinematic motion میں دکھانا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic shot of [SUBJECT] in [SCENE], smoothly transitioning from standing to sitting or sitting to standing with realistic balance, weight transfer, joint motion, and consistent clothing.
**Camera:** Medium full-body stabilized framing.
**Motion:** Continuous controlled posture transition.
**Lighting:** Soft motivated scene lighting.
**Avoid:** No floating body, no broken knees or joints, no teleporting pose, no clothing morphing.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 9:16 by default; 16:9 when requested.

## 8. /characterinteraction
**Purpose:** دو یا زیادہ کرداروں کے درمیان natural interaction اور spatial continuity قائم رکھنا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic interaction between [SUBJECT] and another character in [SCENE], with natural eye lines, gestures, spacing, and responsive body language while preserving each character's identity and clothing.
**Camera:** Medium two-shot or gentle over-the-shoulder framing.
**Motion:** Coordinated but natural conversational movement.
**Lighting:** Consistent motivated key and ambient fill for all characters.
**Avoid:** No identity swapping, no merged bodies, no duplicated limbs, no impossible eye lines, no clothing changes.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 16:9 by default; 9:16 when requested.

## 9. /heroentrance
**Purpose:** کردار کے cinematic entrance کو controlled movement اور strong composition کے ساتھ نمایاں کرنا۔
**Prompt:** Create an 8-second cinematic hero entrance for [SUBJECT] entering [SCENE], using a controlled reveal, confident natural movement, consistent identity, and a strong sense of spatial arrival without changing the character's defining attributes.
**Camera:** Low-to-medium stabilized push-in or reveal.
**Motion:** Natural forward entrance with measured pacing.
**Lighting:** Motivated directional key with subtle rim separation.
**Avoid:** No identity morphing, no exaggerated anatomy, no teleporting entrance, no random costume changes.
**Format:** 8 seconds, 9:16 by default; 16:9 when requested.
